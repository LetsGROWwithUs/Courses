// Common questions

FAQ

Are the sessions 1:1 or group? +
Both are available. Most learners choose 1:1 for a fully personalised pace and project-focused mentoring; small-group batches are offered for select modules on request.
How do I get started? +
Just send a WhatsApp message or fill the contact / enquiry form with your learning goal. We'll get back within 24 hours and align on the right module, scope, and schedule for you.
Do you help with certification prep? +
Yes — targeted prep for exams like C_SAC_2302, C_BW4H_214, C_HANAIMP_18 and more, covering topic-wise revision, practice questions and exam strategy.
Which SAP modules do you cover? +
SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC), BPC, Datasphere, BW/4HANA, SAP MM, S/4HANA, and SAP Business Data Cloud (BDC). Click any module in the "Pick a module" section above to open its detailed reference guide.
I'm a complete fresher — is this for me? +
Absolutely. Programs are designed for freshers, career switchers and working professionals alike. We start from fundamentals and build up to real project scenarios.
Do you also help with job support and freelance projects? +
Yes — live project assistance, mock interviews, resume guidance and freelance project connections are all part of the offering. Just mention what you need in the enquiry form.
